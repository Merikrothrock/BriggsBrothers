Who We Are

Grown by farmers, not sold by a warehouse.

Briggs Brothers Sod Farms has been family-owned and operated for three generations, delivering the finest Fescue and Bluegrass sod to homeowners, builders, landscapers, schools, and parks throughout the Kansas City area.

As proud members of the Turfgrass Producers International (TPI), we hold ourselves to a higher standard — collaborating with university researchers and industry suppliers to make sure every roll of sod that leaves our farm is ready to thrive.
